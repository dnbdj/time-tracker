## Time Tracker v1.4.2

- Task time is now editable in edit mode: −15m / −1m / +1m / +15m steppers
  plus a custom hours:minutes setter (running timers keep running)
- Time never drops below zero; manual edits save across restarts like the rest

**Download:** `time-tracker-1.4.2.apk` below, or the stable link on the
[download page](https://dnbdj.github.io/time-tracker/).
